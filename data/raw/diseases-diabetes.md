A person with diabetes is called a diabetic.
Diabetes could be: 

Type 1 diabetes
Diabetes mellitus (type 1, T1D) - autoimmune disease affecting the pancreas, hormones, which usually requires insulin injections
Type 2 diabetes
Diabetes mellitus (type 2, T2D) - usually caused by diet or obesity
Diabetes insipidus - caused by the hormone vasopressin. Causes increased thirst and urination


Other Types of diabetes
Latent autoimmune diabetes (LADA)
Maturity onset diabetes of the young (MODY)
