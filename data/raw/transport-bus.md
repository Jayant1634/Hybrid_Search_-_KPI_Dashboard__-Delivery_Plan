A bus is a large wheeled vehicle meant to carry many passengers along with the bus driver. It is larger than a car or van, but smaller than the average rail transport. They are most often used for public transport, but they are also in use for charters, and some are privately owned. Although the average bus carries between 30 and 100 passengers, some buses can carry up to 300 passengers. The most common type is the single-deck rigid bus, with double-decker and articulated buses carrying more passengers, and midibuses and minibuses carrying less passengers. Coaches are used for long-distance services. Many types of buses, such as public transport buses, charge a fare. Other types, such as school buses or shuttle buses within a college or university campus, are free. In many jurisdictions, bus drivers require a special large vehicle licence above and beyond a regular driving license. There are many types of bus around the world, Buses primarily replaced horse-drawn omnibuses and stagecoaches in cities during the early to mid-20th century. They offered a more flexible, lower-cost alternative to rail, allowing for flexible routing and avoiding expensive track maintenance, marking a transition from fixed-track urban transit to rubber-tired transport.
Buses are an important part of public transport in places all over the world. Many people who do not have cars, especially in developing countries, use buses to get around. Buses make it easy for them to get to where they want to go. Buses have many uses, including transport inside towns or cities, transport between towns and cities, transport to schools and tourism. Some privately owned buses are used in political campaigns or to transport musicians.
The name "bus" is shorthand for Latin language omnibus, "for everyone". The first omnibuses were horse-drawn coaches, and the name "omnibus" dates to 1823 in France, as a nickname for a service started by mill and spa owner Stanislas Baudry. Steam buses were used for a short time in the 1830s. The first motor-powered buses were made in 1895 in Germany, invented by Karl Benz.
There are many types of bus. For example: a single decker bus or a double decker bus.
A place where people wait for a local bus is called a bus stop. A building where people wait for a long-distance bus or where many buses meet is called a bus station.
